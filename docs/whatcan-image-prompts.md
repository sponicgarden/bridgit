# Image prompts for "What can Bridget do?" cards

One image per card on the More › What can Bridget do? screen.
To make an image, paste the **Style** section, then that card's **Content** line, into the image tool.
Save each image under the file name given (it matches the card's share-link anchor), for example `take-a-photo.png`.

---

## Style (paste first, every time)

```text
Flat editorial illustration for a card in an Android app, landscape 3:2, shown about 360 px wide on a phone, so it must read at a glance at small size.
Palette: confident blue (#1F5BD6) as the main colour, a deep navy (#0B1E4A) for outlines and shadows, soft sky blue (#D6E2FF) and pale blue-grey (#E6EBF5) for backgrounds, white highlights, and one small warm accent of amber (#F2A93B) used only on the single most important detail. No other colours.
Style: clean vector shapes, gentle rounded corners, thin even navy outlines, soft flat shading with at most two tones per shape, light paper-grain texture, generous empty space around one clear focal point. Friendly and calm, a little playful, never cartoonish or childish. Think modern tech documentation illustration.
Recurring characters, kept identical across the whole set: (1) "the phone": a modern slim Android phone with a rounded rectangle body, a small round front camera, painted in the main blue; (2) "the agent": a small friendly floating rounded-square helper with two dot eyes and a soft glow, deep navy with a sky-blue face, representing an AI assistant somewhere else; (3) "the owner": a simple adult figure with no facial detail, plain clothes, gender-neutral, seen in context. Agents and the phone are linked, when relevant, by a thin dashed blue line with small dots travelling along it (the private connection).
Composition: one simple scene, centred, readable as a silhouette; background a soft rounded blob or plain tint, not a full environment.
Do not include: any words, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, UI text, real app icons, watermarks, photographic detail, gradients that band, 3D renders, or people's faces. Nothing scary or surveillance-like: the mood is helpful and consented, the owner is always in control.
```

Notes

- 45 cards. If a card is added to `WhatCanData.kt`, add a row here with the same file-name rule: the title in lower case, every run of other characters replaced by one hyphen.
- The same style block keeps the set consistent. If one image drifts (extra colours, text appearing), regenerate it with the line "Strictly follow the style above; no text of any kind." added at the end.
- Dark mode: the app shows cards on a dark navy background at night. The pale backgrounds in these images will look like light tiles there, which is fine; if you'd rather they blend in, also make a dark set by adding "Dark version: background deep navy (#1A2130), outlines light sky blue (#ADC6FF)."
