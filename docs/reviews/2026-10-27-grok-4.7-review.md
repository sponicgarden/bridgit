# Review: bridgit-spec-v2.md by Grok 4.7 (reasoning high)

- Date: 27 Oct 2026. Reviewer: `grok-4.7`, reasoning effort high, Grok CLI, read-only, no tools; 17 min.
- Reviewed version: commit `40870f2f` (before decision D17, which moved screenshots to an Accessibility service).
- Checked by Claude against the spec and the Pixel the same day. Notes:
  - #6 (screen-share grants) is largely superseded by D17; its points still apply to the fallback (CAP-26b).
  - #3 (IPv6): not reproduced. The phone has no Tailscale IPv6 address on tun0, and `curl -6` via MagicDNS reached Bridgit (401). Keep as a [Verify].
  - #8 (torch needs CAMERA permission): not confirmed by official docs; test on the Pixel when built. Torch/camera mutual exclusion is real.
  - #21 (self-dial): the phone **can** reach its own Tailscale address (shell test: 401 with a Host header). The other parts of #21 (persist an unacknowledged prompt, FLAG_SECURE on pairing) stand.
  - #16 (agents read each other's log): conflicts with owner decision D12; needs an owner call.
  - Side finding: a bare HTTP/1.0 request without a Host header gets 500 from Ktor (never logged). Real clients send Host; low.

- **Applied in spec 2.1 (27 Oct 2026):** all findings except #3 and #8 (kept as [Verify]) and #16 (owner call Q15). New owner calls: Q15 log visibility, Q16 Developer switch, Q17 clipboard approval; Q2 and Q10 reworded.

---

The spec is strong enough to build the direct-mode bridge from, and it already gets several hard Android constraints right: degraded foreground-service types after a background restart, restricted settings, screenshot consent, and selected-photos detection. It is not safe to implement as written. Relay “end-to-end” encryption is derivable by the relay, a handful of Decided principles contradict later capabilities, and several Android 14+ rules are missing or misstated in ways that will wedge setup, battery, screen capture, health reads, downloads, or the visibility promise. P1 is also far too large for one release, while per-agent isolation and screenshot consent sit too late relative to the risk.

| 1 | Critical | ARC-14, ARC-15, SEC-02, ARC-18 | Relay mode does not provide end-to-end encryption.
