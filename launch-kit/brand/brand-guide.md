# BRIDGIT brand system

## Position

BRIDGIT is an Android appliance platform for authorized AI agents. Promise: give an agent controlled access to a real device, then let it assemble useful behavior. Audience: people who use agents and have a phone to put to work. Secondary audience: developers connecting agent tools. Avoid leading with jargon such as MCP; describe the protocol in developer materials.

Primary line: **Turn any Android device into an agentic appliance.** Supporting line: **Turn a spare Android into something useful.** Explanation: **Your agent builds the workflow. BRIDGIT runs it on your phone.** Current-build version: **Connect your agent to your Android camera, microphone and photos.**

Do not say "any chatbot works instantly", "never misses an emergency", "always online", "Google approved", "works on every old Android" or "your data never leaves your phone". It intentionally transfers authorized media. Say "any compatible agent you authorize", describe connectivity and OS limitations, and separate current from planned features.

## Visual identity

Original bridge/beacon symbol: two upright endpoints joined by a low arch, with a signal dot above the right endpoint. The bridge represents a working connection between agent and physical device. Use the supplied SVG masters, not a raster trace. Do not use a medical cross, baby icon or robot mascot as the core mark; BRIDGIT serves many appliances.

Ink #142C32; warm white #F5F2E9; signal lime #D8F36A; teal #1B6A64; orange #E58D57. Ink on lime and warm white is the primary readable pairing. Teal is an accent with dark-on-light text. Status uses a word and icon in addition to color; orange is not the only warning signal. Do not use low-contrast lime text on white.

System sans: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, sans-serif. Display: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif. Code: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace. All are local system fallbacks; no external font requests or bundled font license obligations introduced.

Clear space: at least one signal-dot diameter on all sides. Minimum mark 24px; minimum wordmark 112px wide. Never stretch, add shadows, place the signal dot elsewhere or change the geometry. Icon export is a full square; let Play apply its own corner treatment. Favicon simplifies to the mark. Do not change the installed Android launcher icon as part of this documentation task.

## Voice

Useful, direct, capable. "Choose what this phone can do." "Your agent proposed a change." "Camera session paused. Open BRIDGIT to resume." Describe actual state; avoid reassuring language when disconnected. "Anna acknowledged your alert" is valid only after Anna's active response, not a network acknowledgment.
