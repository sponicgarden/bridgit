Live text today

A window into your agent’s work

Audio + optional transcription

A voice notebook

“Record this idea and turn it into a note.”

Record up to two minutes, with optional transcription on phones that have a compatible offline recognizer. Your connected agent summarizes and organizes the note.

Camera + on-device OCR

A desk scanner

“Send a picture of this receipt to my agent.”

Capture a receipt, then extract Latin-script text on the phone. Snapshots require an unlocked phone and camera access; your agent handles filing and follow-through.

Speech playback today

A talking checklist

“Read me the next step while my hands are busy.”

Your agent speaks a recipe step, packing item or workshop instruction through the phone. Your agent must manage any schedule; Bridgit does not include an alarm scheduler.

Interactive cards

A family message board

“Put the family’s plans on this spare phone.”

Display an agent-created card with useful information. Your agent must manage refreshes; there is no dedicated household-board runtime.

Camera + agent-managed notes

A project photo diary

“Take a picture of my project and keep a progress note.”

Capture a project photo on request while the phone is unlocked. Your agent stores it with context and manages any schedule; a dedicated on-device diary is not included.

Audio tools today · Agent-led practice

A language-practice companion

“Say a phrase, listen to my reply and help me improve.”

Use short audio recordings and speech playback with a connected tutoring agent. The agent supplies language understanding; continuous conversation is not built in.

A guided city walk

“Tell me about the places I’m walking past.”

City tour shares location, heading and photos you take with one connected agent, which supplies the commentary. Pause or end the session from your phone. It is not continuous camera streaming.

Where the product stands

Implemented tools.
Clear limits.

The 1.6.0 API reference documents camera and audio tools, video, on-device text recognition, optional offline transcription, file exchange, sensors, phone controls and per-agent permissions. The catalog distinguishes those implemented capabilities from four exploratory ideas.

Device acceptance testing is partial. Hardware, Android access and installed recognition services affect availability. Check your app’s live capabilities before using a tool. Earlier app builds and the 1.5.4 screenshots show a smaller set.

A few things to know.

Can I use my own AI agent?

Yes, when its tools or connector can reach your Tailscale network and call the Bridgit HTTP API or MCP endpoint. A model subscription or a logo on this page does not establish that connection. Read the developer guide.

How old can the phone be?

Bridgit currently requires Android 14 or later. Older-version support is under consideration and is not available or guaranteed. Individual features also depend on the phone’s hardware and Android access.

Does everything stay on the phone?

No. Requested content goes to your authorized agents, which may retain it. Device controls and activity history live on the phone. Crash reporting is on by default and can be turned off; update checks, configured callbacks and audio downloads also make network requests. Read the privacy policy and deletion limits.

Does an agent get unrestricted control?

No. It can call the tools your app exposes within feature switches, Android permissions and its grants. Review unrestricted legacy agents and choose narrower grants where supported. Cards can ask for your answer; they are not a blanket approval of later real-world actions.

Can I rely on it in an emergency?

No. Bridgit is not emergency dispatch, medical monitoring or a safety alarm. Proposed monitoring and family-help appliances are not available. Never depend on an agent response or notification to summon emergency help.

Can I get it on Google Play?

Not yet. This website has no installer or signup form and collects no email addresses through a form. Existing preview users can check the in-app updater. Support can receive questions; there is no promised release date or automatic enrollment.
