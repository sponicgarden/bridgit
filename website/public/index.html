A few things to know.

Can I use my own AI agent?

Yes, if it can reach the phone’s private network and call the BRIDGIT API, directly or through a compatible tool. BRIDGIT does not require one AI vendor. Pairing and device permissions stay under your control.

How old can the phone be?

The current build requires Android 14 or later. We are designing support for Android 10 and later, with capability checks for each phone. Older-device support is not yet available or guaranteed.

Does everything stay on the phone?

Device controls and the activity log live on the phone. Requested photos and audio go to your authorized agent. The proposed workflow runtime executes locally, but contacting agents or family members still needs a working connection.

Can I rely on it in an emergency?

The proposed help station is an additional way to contact family, not an emergency dispatch or medical monitoring service. Power, connectivity and phrase detection can fail. It is not available in the current build.

Can I get it on Google Play?

Not yet. You can download the Android APK directly or request a link by email. The Google Play release is still being prepared.
