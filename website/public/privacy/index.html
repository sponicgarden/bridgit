BRIDGIT privacy policy draft

Draft for product review — not the published release policy. The verified privacy contact, domain, publisher details and final release behavior must be confirmed before public publication or Play submission.

Prepared 28 October 2026. Intended publisher: Sponic Garden, Inc., subject to confirmation of the publishing entity. This draft describes the current personal BRIDGIT Android bridge and identifies planned features separately.

Device access and transfers

BRIDGIT can access camera photos, microphone clips and gallery images only within Android permissions and the capabilities enabled on the phone. It also handles agent names, credential hashes, request logs and device status. Requested media and API results are transmitted to the authorized agent. The current /v1/audit API lets authenticated agents retrieve request logs across agents.

Storage and retention

Agent records and the audit log reside in app-private storage. The current log periodically trims to the newest 1,000 entries and can temporarily contain up to about 1,100 entries. Temporary camera and audio files are deleted in request cleanup; interruption can affect cleanup and must be tested for the release. Original gallery media remains in Android storage. App backup is disabled. Removing an agent deletes its stored credential record but does not automatically erase historical audit entries. Clear the log separately if desired.

Recipients and network

The current direct connection uses the owner’s Tailscale network. Tailscale has its own network service and privacy practices. Agent services you choose can process and retain media under their own policies. BRIDGIT does not control deletion at those recipients. This draft does not claim that authorized media stays on the phone or that every third party receives no metadata.

Analytics and advertising

The reviewed 1.2 source contains no advertising or session-replay SDK and no product analytics service. Optional diagnostics, hosted relays, signup forms, streaming and contact-alert services are not covered as implemented current features. They require updated disclosures before release.

Control and deletion

You can stop the listener, disable capabilities, revoke Android permissions, rotate/remove agent keys, and clear the local audit log. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling BRIDGIT removes app-local records; it does not delete gallery originals, previously exported logs or copies retained by agents.

Children and other people

The intended audience is adults configuring devices they own or are authorized to manage. Capture affects other people nearby; the operator must obtain appropriate knowledge and consent. This is not a child-directed account service. Future baby-monitor or help-station functionality will require feature-specific disclosures.

This website preview

The site code has no analytics, marketing forms, session replay or external font requests. The hosting provider necessarily processes network requests and may retain operational logs under its own practices. A future public policy must identify the production host and applicable retention.

Contact and rights

A verified privacy mailbox and the publisher’s applicable contact details are pending. The public release must provide that contact and an appropriate rights-request process for its markets. This draft is not an active request channel.
