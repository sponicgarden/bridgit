Bridgit / Your data

Your Bridgit data.

Stop new access, clear local records and understand which copies remain elsewhere.

Stop new access

On the phone, pause the listener, switch off a capability, or remove or rotate an agent’s key. Where supported, revoke individual capability grants. Later requests are refused according to those controls.

The 1.6.0 implementation also cancels guarded operations after relevant access changes: capture switches apply to capture requests; key, agent and grant checks apply more broadly. This does not guarantee cancellation of every Android operation or recall a photo, file or message already delivered. Timed setting restoration may be delayed by Android restrictions; force-stop can prevent restoration until the app runs again.

Delete records stored on the phone

Use Activity’s clear-history control for request history. That does not erase every interaction, feedback or diagnostic record. In newer builds, use Device tools to delete received files, shares and selected Downloads. Transfers have 30-day retention with cleanup checks; removing an agent revokes access and its transfers are cleaned up when the transfer store is accessed. It does not erase all historical logs.

For a complete local reset, clear Bridgit’s Android app storage or uninstall it. This removes app-local records, settings and pairing. You will need to pair agents again. Back up anything you intentionally want to retain before choosing that reset.

Understand the other retention limits

Activity defaults to 30 days and 20,000 entries, with periodic trimming and configurable limits. Notification text is held in memory and cleared on pause, disable or disconnect. Bridgit does not retain Health Connect source records. Clipboard approval text expires or is removed after a decision, with old status records pruned later. See the privacy policy for details.

What local deletion does not erase

Original gallery photos and videos, exported files and logs, content already transferred to agents, support email and crash reports already sent remain outside a local app reset. Delete external copies through Android storage, the export destination, or the agent/provider that holds them. Turning crash reporting off stops new reporting; it does not recall past reports.

Information held by the publisher

Email support@bridgit.cc about information held in support or diagnostic systems. Describe the request without sending keys or unnecessary personal media. We may need proportionate information to identify the record. We can act on information we hold, not every agent’s copies.

No Bridgit online account

The current app does not create a Bridgit online account, and this page is not an account-deletion form. Any future hosted account service will need its own clearly published controls and retention details.

Control build records

In the local control build, open Agent control and appliances from Home’s menu. Stop all agent work, revoke an agent’s individual grants, revoke a viewer or recipient, remove selected local files, or remove completed platform history. These controls do not erase original gallery media or copies already exported or delivered. Clearing completed history retains idempotency tombstones, so an old request cannot silently run again.
