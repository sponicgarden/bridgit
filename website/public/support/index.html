BRIDGIT support

Make the state of the phone easy to understand.

Current build requirements

Android 14 or later, a private Tailscale connection and a compatible API-capable agent. You can download the current APK or request a link by email. There is no published Play listing yet. Older-device compatibility and appliance workflows remain in development.

Agent cannot connect

Open BRIDGIT on the device. Check that Tailscale is connected, the listener is enabled and the named agent has a current key. Never send your key or personal photos in a support report. Do not open the listener to the public internet as a workaround.

Camera or microphone unavailable

Open BRIDGIT once after reboot or interruption, check Android permissions and confirm the capability is enabled. Snapshots currently require an unlocked phone. Another app or Android privacy controls can occupy or block the sensor.

Stop access or remove data

Stop the listener or disable the capability on the phone. Remove or rotate the agent key to block future calls. See your data controls for local deletion and agent-side copies.

Report a problem

Contact support@bridgit.cc for help. A useful report includes app version, Android version, phone model, feature, time, observed result and request ID. Share logs only after removing private data. Do not include tokens, QR codes, personal audio or screenshots.
