Bridgit / Support

Help with your Bridgit preview.

Check the phone’s state, find the missing permission, and get your agent connected again.

Requirements and availability

Android 14 or later, your Tailscale network and a compatible HTTP- or MCP-capable agent are required. Feature support depends on your installed build and hardware. Bridgit is not on Google Play; this website has no installer or signup form. Existing preview users can check the app’s updater.

Agent cannot connect

Open Bridgit, check Tailscale, enable the listener and confirm the named agent has a current key. The agent host or connector must be able to reach the phone’s private address. The marketing website is not that address. Do not expose the listener to the public internet.

Camera or microphone unavailable

Open Bridgit once after reboot or interruption. Check Android permissions, notifications, feature switches and the agent’s grants. Snapshots require an unlocked phone. Other apps and Android privacy controls can block sensors. Video with audio needs both camera and microphone access.

Text recognition or transcription unavailable

On-device text recognition supports Latin script. Transcription requires a compatible Android on-device recognizer, an installed offline language and file-input support. It has no cloud-recognizer fallback. An implemented API can still return not supported on your device.

Special Android access

Notification text, app-usage summaries, Health Connect, screen sharing and some device controls each need their relevant Android access. Use Access or Device tools to review it. Health Connect is read-only; notifications are active texts in memory, not an archive. Clipboard requests require your foreground Copy tap.

Files and timed controls

Received files, shares and selected Downloads have separate histories in Device tools. The owner chooses whether to open or export a file. If a timed sound or display change did not restore, review Device tools and use Undo after restoring any required Android access. Scheduling can be delayed by Android restrictions; force-stop can prevent restoration until the app runs again.

Stop access or remove data

Pause the listener, disable a feature or revoke an agent’s key or grants. Your data controls explain local deletion and copies already sent to agents.

Contact support

Email support@bridgit.cc. Include app version from Settings, Android version, phone model, feature, time, observed result and request ID. Remove private data from logs. Do not send tokens, pairing codes, QR codes, personal audio or screenshots.
