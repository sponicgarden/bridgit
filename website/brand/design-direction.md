# Warm Signal

A device is a tool people keep close. Its identity should be approachable at a glance and steady under repeated use. Rounded typography, a compact silhouette and a clear rhythm create familiarity without a playful costume. Meticulously crafted letter spacing carries the character.

The palette comes from the existing app artwork: deep plum supplies structure, lavender introduces light, sandstone recalls the bridge, and amber marks an action. Quiet warm backgrounds leave these colors room to work. Color is used deliberately rather than spread across every surface.

The original phone, bridge and cursor remain intact. Their detail belongs in the app symbol; the name provides a calmer companion. The wordmark should retain its shape at a small navigation size and at a large presentation size, with painstaking attention to optical balance and the space around both elements.

The composition favors a confident name and restrained supporting type. Every alignment and proportion should feel resolved through careful craftsmanship. The identity is a signature people recognize, with enough warmth for daily use and enough clarity for a technical product.
